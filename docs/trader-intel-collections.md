# Trader Intel collections — methodology v2

These anonymous GET routes read prepared Redis rankings. The background collector reads **only local PostgreSQL wallet-intel observations and catalog/quote tables**. It does not call Polymarket, a blockchain RPC or a model, or run wallet-intel itself.

| Endpoint                           | What the card means                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/intel/traders/big-moves`         | “Position grew by approximately $X.” X is the increase in observed shares times the local observation price. It is not a purchase, an execution price, or proof of cash spent.     |
| `/intel/traders/top-earners-today` | Estimated net P&L on the observed, costed portion of positions over the trailing 24h, including losses and partial exits. It is not total wallet income or a statement after fees. |
| `/intel/traders/winning-streaks`   | Consecutive qualifying profitable completed positions inferred from the tracked history in this category, not the wallet's lifetime win rate. A loss interrupts the sequence.      |

Supply `?category=sports` to limit a tab to one group. There are at most three distinct owner families per category. Top Earners Today and Winning Streaks share the same shortlist of up to 100 wallets from a recent pool of up to 2,000. Both rank every wallet already evaluated in the current window, using one historical calculation per wallet. On a partial publication, `diagnostics.observedWallets` can be smaller than `selectedWallets`. The last 32 activity entries supply hints (closures and large increases), interleaved with recency. Hints alone never make a card.

Big Moves independently scans the trailing 24 hours of public wallet-intel deltas, not that shortlist. It ranks by observed USD increase, then recency, with deterministic ties. Both share observations must be in the window and match the instrument. Technical zeroes, private fills, infrastructure addresses and suspected market makers are not evidence of a trader increasing a position. Follow-only data cannot make a private wallet discoverable. The increase must be at least $500, remain directionally open at the latest observation, and have a fresh canonical ask within 5¢ and 10% of the observed price. A profitable month or a known typical stake is **not** required. “Position grew by approximately $X” is not a claim about an executed purchase.

The Big Moves response has `coverage.universe: all_public_wallet_intel` and `diagnostics.scope: all_public_position_increases`. Counts refer to wallets with candidate increases, not to every wallet ever registered. `relativeToTypical`, realized profit, history length and completed positions can be null when not calculated. Read the opportunity from `achievement.instrument` and `achievement.quote`; a particular large move need not be the trader’s most recent increase. The other two endpoints keep their existing economics and coverage fields.

### Method and limits

Every newly prepared instrument has `eventTitle` (the full local parent event title or null) in addition to its unchanged market `title`, side and IDs. Render them together when different. An aggregate without a featured position is not tied to a made-up event. Older still-serving snapshots may omit the new fields.

Winning Streaks adds `achievement.latestWinInstrument` and `achievement.recentResults` (newest first, up to ten **observed, costed closes in the category**, not ten executed trades). Example: a completed episode can have `result: "loss", estimatedEpisodePnlUsd: "-3.25", qualifiesForStreak: false`. Show wins, losses and break-evens differently; unfilled slots are **unknown**, never losses. The raw strip need not match the qualifying streak (dust, entry-price and break-even rules). Equal timestamps are simultaneous; presentation ties do not establish a trading sequence.

`GET /wallets/:walletId` additionally returns `wallet.recentCloses` for the wallet across categories. Example ready shape: `{status:"ready",windowStart,windowEnd,coverage:"observed_only"|"known_gaps",items:[{instrument,completedAt,estimatedEpisodePnlUsd,result:"win"|"loss"|"flat",qualifiesForStreak}]}`. This uses the retained public Polymarket activity and local frozen market bindings (not limited to the ranking’s 30 days) for any Polygon wallet, including traders outside the earnings/streaks shortlist. A partial reduction alone is not a close. The basis distinguishes an inferred local binary settlement from an observed full exit. The period begins at the first retained public delta (null when none); it does not promise lifetime coverage. An unknown basis is never a zero-dollar result. Use `windowEnd` as the as-of time, not the request time.

The first profile visit starts a bounded local reader; wallets alternate after each page or catalog batch. On a cold profile the base profile remains available: `preparing`, `busy` (local reader saturated) or `unavailable` (temporary failure) have `items:null`; refresh the same profile with backoff (e.g. 5, 15, then 30 seconds while visible). Other chains return `unsupported`; do not poll. Only `ready` with `items:[]` means no costed closures observed in this window. Profile cache hits still check the independently refreshed result. No provider calls, trades or authenticated account fills are used.

The 14-day check uses observed public activity, not wallet registration age. On first seeing an existing balance, a local snapshot mark may anchor its estimated cost (`snapshot_mark_basis`); first sight alone is not a Big Move. A sampling gap alone never establishes a purchase, sale or hedge unwind. Open losses use local bid marks when available. `positionAsOf` is the last observed balance time, not the ranking publication time. For streaks, `lengthIsLowerBound` means “at least N observed wins”; it never supplies an unobserved win. All numeric cutoffs remain unchanged.

- Local snapshot deltas include the previous and new share count, observed mark and timestamp. Changes in price with unchanged shares never create Big Moves. Private order fills are not counted again. The local profile's weighted-average ledger estimates P&L; fees and activity between snapshots are not known. An episode without a reconcilable entry is omitted from estimated income and cannot create a winning close. `unknown_cost_basis_omitted` marks incomplete coverage. A missing/synthetic zero is not a profitable exit. A locally recorded, final binary result may settle a remaining estimated position as of its recorded resolution time.
- Earnings and Streaks require at least 14 days of observed history, 20 completed positions and a positive category result with losses considered. Streaks require at least five qualifying wins; dust (<$10), break-even and entries above 90¢ do not qualify. Tied win/loss observations do not establish order. Unknown history does not create a win.
- `methodologyVersion: 2`; `policyVersion: 2` for the global Big Moves scan, `1` for Earnings/Streaks. The achievement kinds and route names stay the same. Big Moves returns `estimatedIncreaseUsd`, `observationPrice`, `observedAt`, and shares before/after instead of fill/spend fields. Earnings uses `estimatedNetPnl24hUsd`, streaks `estimatedStreakPnlUsd`. Decimal amounts are strings. Position quotes are informational and expire independently.
- Coverage basis: `observed_position_deltas`, `estimated_realizations`, or `inferred_closed_positions`. `diagnostics` separates selected, attempted, pending, observed, and qualified wallets; source failures are not economic exclusions.
- The earnings/streaks collector traverses local activity in timestamp/UUID order in 1,000-row pages. Its lease fences the calculation and cursor; a restart can resume. Big Moves publishes its own complete window on a separate lease and cache key, so a slow portfolio cannot delay it. No database work occurs on a public GET.
- Read `availability` before rendering. `ranked` means a real ranking (possibly empty); `preparing` means a new frozen shortlist has been saved, and `between_windows` means the last ranking expired. During either handoff the response has no cards or ranking diagnostics; show a loading state. If neither window nor handoff is available, or Redis is unavailable, return 503 with Retry-After. Cached conditional reads support ETag/304.

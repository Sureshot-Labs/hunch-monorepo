# Trader Intel collections — methodology v2

These anonymous GET routes read prepared Redis rankings. The background collector reads **only local PostgreSQL wallet-intel observations and catalog/quote tables**. It does not call Polymarket, a blockchain RPC or a model, or run wallet-intel itself.

| Endpoint                           | What the card means                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/intel/traders/big-moves`         | “Position grew by approximately $X.” X is the increase in observed shares times the local observation price. It is not a purchase, an execution price, or proof of cash spent.     |
| `/intel/traders/top-earners-today` | Estimated net P&L on the observed, costed portion of positions over the trailing 24h, including losses and partial exits. It is not total wallet income or a statement after fees. |
| `/intel/traders/winning-streaks`   | Consecutive qualifying profitable completed positions inferred from the tracked history in this category, not the wallet's lifetime win rate. A loss interrupts the sequence.      |

Supply `?category=sports` to limit a tab to one group. There are at most three distinct owner families in each category. A partial or empty ranking is valid. The collector examines up to 50 active system-tracked Polymarket wallets from a bounded 2,000-row recent pool, with existing historical metrics/MM exclusions. The last 32 activity entries per wallet supply selection hints: observed closures and large buys/share increases, interleaved with recency. Hints alone never make a card.

### Method and limits

The 14-day check uses observed public activity, not wallet registration age. On first seeing an existing balance, a local snapshot mark may anchor its estimated cost (`snapshot_mark_basis`); first sight alone is not a Big Move. A sampling gap alone never establishes a purchase, sale or hedge unwind. Open losses use local bid marks when available. `positionAsOf` is the last observed balance time, not the ranking publication time. For streaks, `lengthIsLowerBound` means “at least N observed wins”; it never supplies an unobserved win. All numeric cutoffs remain unchanged.

- Local snapshot deltas include the previous and new share count, observed mark and timestamp. Changes in price with unchanged shares never create Big Moves. Private order fills are not counted again. The local profile's weighted-average ledger estimates P&L; fees and activity between snapshots are not known. An episode without a reconcilable entry is omitted from estimated income and cannot create a winning close. `unknown_cost_basis_omitted` marks incomplete coverage. A missing/synthetic zero is not a profitable exit. A locally recorded, final binary result may settle a remaining estimated position as of its recorded resolution time.
- The numeric policy is unchanged: at least 14 days of observed history, 20 completed positions, positive category result with losses considered. Big Moves uses the trailing 24h, at least $500 estimated increase, at least one typical prior category stake, remaining directional exposure, a current canonical market and a fresh ask within 5¢ and 10% of the observation price. Streaks require at least five qualifying wins; dust (<$10), break-even and entries above 90¢ do not qualify. Tied win/loss observations do not establish order. Unknown history does not create a win.
- `methodologyVersion: 2`, `policyVersion: 1`. The achievement kinds and route names stay the same. Big Moves returns `estimatedIncreaseUsd`, `observationPrice`, `observedAt`, and shares before/after instead of fill/spend fields. Earnings uses `estimatedNetPnl24hUsd`, streaks `estimatedStreakPnlUsd`. Common amounts are `estimatedClosedPnl30dUsd` and `estimatedCategoryClosedPnl30dUsd`; an unknown whole-wallet estimate may be null. Decimal amounts are strings. Position quotes are informational and expire independently.
- Coverage basis: `observed_position_deltas`, `estimated_realizations`, or `inferred_closed_positions`. `diagnostics` separates selected, attempted, pending, observed, and qualified wallets; source failures are not economic exclusions.
- Local activity is traversed in timestamp/UUID order in 1,000-row pages. A lease fences the compact calculation and exact cursor to the wallet and frozen window. A restart can resume; a prefix never becomes a finished ranking. All three tabs publish atomically. Old methodology cache keys are not reused.
- Read `availability` before rendering. `ranked` means a real ranking (possibly empty); `preparing` means a new frozen shortlist has been saved, and `between_windows` means the last ranking expired. During either handoff the response has no cards or ranking diagnostics; show a loading state. If neither window nor handoff is available, or Redis is unavailable, return 503 with Retry-After. Cached conditional reads support ETag/304.
